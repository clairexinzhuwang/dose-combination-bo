records the OSA dose-availability sensitivity: two protocols, two strata,
three policies, two probability cutoffs and 200 replicate identifiers.

there is no externally timestamped pre-run commit. Current results and their
relationship to the main paper are in [the sensitivity summary](protocol_scaffold_sensitivity.md).
